Budget High Protein Meals That Fit a Real Grocery Budget

Protein Pennies helps students, young professionals and anyone on a tight weekly food budget eat more protein without overspending. Every recipe lists its protein and its cost per serving.

Where to start

  • High protein meals under $10: six recipes, each under $3 a serving.
  • Cheap protein sources for meal prep: what to buy, ranked by protein per dollar.
  • Budget high protein meal prep for the week: a five-day plan over 60 g of protein a day.

How we price meals

Costs use typical grocery store prices for staple ingredients, divided by the number of servings. Swap a pricey protein for a cheaper one and the cost drops, which each page explains.
